Cookie Policy

What cookies and similar technologies this website uses.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local or session storage, work in a comparable way.

2. What we use

This website does not use advertising, marketing or analytics cookies, and we do not track visitors across other websites. The website is designed to work without cookies for ordinary visitors.

  • Session storage (organisers only). On the GFC Cup page, tournament organisers who sign in to update scores may have a temporary entry kept in session storage so they stay signed in. It is cleared when the browser tab is closed. Ordinary visitors do not use this.
  • Site administration. The club's content management login may store a sign-in token in the browser of authorised administrators. It is not used for visitors.

3. Third-party content

Some content is loaded from other providers, which may set their own cookies or collect technical data such as your IP address:

  • Google Fonts (typefaces used across the site);
  • Google Maps (embedded map on the Contact page);
  • Facebook, Instagram and TikTok (only if you click through to those platforms).

Please see those providers' own policies for details.

4. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking third-party content such as the embedded map may mean some features do not display.

5. Changes

If we add analytics or other tools in future, we will update this policy first. See also our Privacy Policy.

6. Contact

Questions about cookies: gurkhafc2008@yahoo.com.
