Our story

Built on passion. Driven by football.

What began in 2008 as a platform for football enthusiasts to come together has grown into one of the established names in Queensland's Nepalese football community. For more than 18 years, Gurkha FC has proudly represented the Nepalese community across Brisbane, Queensland and interstate competitions throughout Australia.
