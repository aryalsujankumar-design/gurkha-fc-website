Privacy Policy

How Gurkha FC Brisbane handles personal information collected through this website.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Who we are

Gurkha FC ("we", "us", "our") is a Nepalese community football club based in Brisbane, Queensland, Australia, established in 2008. This policy explains how we handle personal information in connection with our website (gurkhafc.com.au). We aim to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

2. Information we collect

  • Information you give us. If you email us or use the contact form, we receive the details you provide, such as your name, email address, the reason for your enquiry and your message.
  • Photos and videos. Our gallery shows photos from club events, matches and tournaments. These may show players, supporters and guests.
  • Technical information. Like any website, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and date and time of access, for security and reliable operation.

We do not run advertising or analytics trackers on this website, and we do not sell personal information.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries about player registration, sponsorship, volunteering, coaching and partnerships.
  • To run and promote club activities and tournaments, including the GFC Cup.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell or rent your information. We may share it only with service providers that help us run the website (for example website hosting by Netlify, and website support by Ceeros Tech), or where the law requires it. Some of these providers are located overseas, so information may be processed outside Australia.

5. Third-party services and links

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and shows a Google Maps location on the Contact page. These services may receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads. Our pages also link to Facebook, Instagram and TikTok; those platforms have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for them.

6. Photos and your image

If you appear in a photo or video on our website and would like it removed, email us at gurkhafc2008@yahoo.com and we will remove it promptly.

7. Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. We keep information only as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected, and then delete or de-identify it where reasonable.

8. Children

Our club welcomes young players. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us on your behalf.

9. Access, correction and complaints

You may ask to access or correct personal information we hold about you, or make a privacy complaint, by emailing gurkhafc2008@yahoo.com. We will respond within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date above.

11. Contact

Gurkha FC, Brisbane, Queensland, Australia. Email: gurkhafc2008@yahoo.com.
